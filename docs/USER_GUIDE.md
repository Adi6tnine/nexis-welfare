# NEXIS User Guide

## Welcome to NEXIS

NEXIS (National Eligibility eXpert and Information System) helps you discover government welfare schemes you're eligible for. This guide will help you get started.

## Getting Started

### 1. Access NEXIS

Visit: **https://nexis.gov.in**

Available in:
- English
- Hindi (हिंदी)

### 2. Choose Your Language

Click the language button in the top right corner to switch between English and Hindi.

## Checking Your Eligibility

### Step 1: Enter Your Information

Click "Check My Eligibility" and provide:

1. **Age**: Your current age
2. **State**: Where you live
3. **Occupation**: Your current occupation
   - Farmer
   - Agricultural Worker
   - Student
   - Unemployed
   - Self-Employed
   - Private Sector
   - Government Employee
   - Daily Wage Worker
   - Artisan
   - Fisherman
   - Other

4. **Annual Income**: Your yearly income in rupees
5. **Gender**: Male, Female, or Other
6. **Social Category**: General, OBC, SC, or ST
7. **Disability**: Check if you have a disability

### Step 2: View Results

You'll see two lists:

**Eligible Schemes** (Green border)
- Schemes you qualify for
- Shows benefits you can receive
- Click "View Details" for more information

**All Schemes** (Gray border)
- All available schemes
- Shows why you don't qualify (if ineligible)

### Step 3: Get AI Explanation

Click on any scheme to:
- Understand why you're eligible or not
- See what documents you need
- Learn how to apply
- Find alternative schemes

## Using the AI Assistant

### Starting a Chat

1. Click "Ask AI Assistant" from the results page
2. Type your question in the chat box
3. Press "Send" or hit Enter

### What You Can Ask

**About Eligibility**
- "What schemes am I eligible for?"
- "Why am I not eligible for [scheme name]?"
- "What would I need to qualify for [scheme name]?"

**About Application**
- "How do I apply for [scheme name]?"
- "What documents do I need?"
- "Where is the nearest application center?"

**About Benefits**
- "How much money will I receive?"
- "When will I get the benefits?"
- "How long does the scheme last?"

### Tips for Better Answers

✅ **Do**:
- Ask specific questions
- Mention scheme names if asking about a specific scheme
- Ask follow-up questions for clarification

❌ **Don't**:
- Ask about schemes from other countries
- Request personal advice (medical, legal, financial)
- Share sensitive personal information

## Managing Your Profile

### Editing Your Profile

1. Click "Edit Profile" from the results page
2. Update any information
3. Click "Check My Eligibility" again

### Clearing Your Data

Click "Clear Form" to start over with a fresh profile.

### Deleting Your Account

To permanently delete all your data:
1. Go to Settings (if available)
2. Click "Delete My Account"
3. Confirm deletion

Your data will be deleted within 24 hours.

## Understanding Results

### Match Score

Each eligible scheme shows a match score (0-100):
- **100**: You meet all criteria perfectly
- **75-99**: You meet most criteria
- **50-74**: You meet some criteria

### Eligibility Status

**Eligible** (Green)
- You meet all requirements
- You can apply for this scheme

**Not Eligible** (Gray)
- You don't meet one or more requirements
- See reasons why you don't qualify

### Scheme Information

Each scheme card shows:
- **Scheme Name**: Official name
- **Description**: What the scheme provides
- **Benefits**: What you'll receive
- **Eligibility**: Who can apply

## Privacy and Security

### Your Data is Safe

- All data is encrypted

- Age, state, occupation, income

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is NEXIS free to use?**  
A: Yes, NEXIS is completely free for all Indian citizens.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**  
A: No, you can check eligibility without creating an account.

**Q: How accurate are the results?**  
A: Results are based on official scheme criteria. Always verify with the scheme authority before applying.

**Q: Can I use NEXIS on my phone?**  
A: Yes, NEXIS works on all devices - phones, tablets, and computers.

### Eligibility Questions

**Q: Why am I not eligible for any schemes?**  
A: This could be due to your age, income, occupation, or location. Try the AI assistant for personalized suggestions.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple schemes?**  
A: Yes, you can apply for all schemes you're eligible for.

**Q: How often are schemes updated?**  
A: Schemes are updated regularly as new programs are announced.

### Application Questions

**Q: Does NEXIS help me apply?**  
A: NEXIS shows you what you're eligible for. You'll need to apply through the official scheme channels.

**Q: Where do I apply?**  
A: Each scheme has its own application process. Check the scheme details or ask the AI assistant.

**Q: What documents do I need?**  
A: Required documents vary by scheme. Use the AI assistant to get a list for specific schemes.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why is the page loading slowly?**  
A: This could be due to your internet connection. Try refreshing the page.

**Q: The AI assistant isn't responding**  
A: Wait a few seconds and try again. If the problem persists, refresh the page.

**Q: I found a bug**  
A: Report it to support@nexis.gov.in with details about what happened.
