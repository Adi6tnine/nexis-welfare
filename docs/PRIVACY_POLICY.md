# NEXIS Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: March 8, 2026

## Introduction

NEXIS (National Eligibility eXpert and Information System) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our service.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you use NEXIS, we collect the following information:

- **Age**: To determine eligibility for age-restricted schemes
- **State of Residence**: To match you with state-specific schemes
- **Occupation**: To identify occupation-based schemes
- **Annual Income**: To determine income-based eligibility
- **Gender**: For gender-specific schemes
- **Social Category**: For category-specific schemes (General, OBC, SC, ST)
- **Disability Status**: For disability-specific schemes

### Usage Information
We automatically collect:

- **Session Data**: Conversation history with AI assistant
- **Eligibility Results**: Schemes you're eligible for
- **Timestamps**: When you use our services
- **Technical Data**: IP address, browser type, device information

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

1. **Check Eligibility**: Match your profile with government schemes
2. **Provide AI Assistance**: Answer questions about schemes
3. **Generate Explanations**: Explain why you are or aren't eligible
4. **Improve Services**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance our system
5. **Ensure Security**: Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

## Data Storage and Security

### Encryption
- **In Transit**: All data is encrypted using TLS 1.2+ during transmission
- **At Rest**: All stored data is encrypted using AWS KMS encryption
- **Database**: DynamoDB tables use server-side encryption (SSE-KMS)
- **Files**: S3 buckets use server-side encryption (SSE-S3)

### Access Control
- Strict access controls using AWS IAM with least privilege principle
- Multi-factor authentication required for administrative access
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

### Data Location
- All data is stored in AWS data centers in India
- Data does not leave Indian territory
- Compliant with Indian data localization requirements

## Data Retention

### Automatic Deletion
- **Eligibility Results**: Deleted after 90 days
- **Chat Sessions**: Deleted after 90 days
- **AI Explanation Cache**: Deleted after 7 days
- **User Profiles**: Deleted after 2 years of inactivity

### Manual Deletion
You can request immediate deletion of your data at any time.

## Your Rights

Under Indian data protection laws, you have the right to:

### 1. Access Your Data
Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

**How to Request**: Contact us at privacy@nexis.gov.in

### 2. Correct Your Data
Update or correct inaccurate information in your profile.

**How to Update**: Use the "Edit Profile" feature in the application

### 3. Delete Your Data
Request complete deletion of your personal information.

**How to Request**: 
- Use the "Delete My Account" feature in settings
- Email privacy@nexis.gov.in
- We will confirm deletion within 24 hours

### 4. Export Your Data
Download all your data in a portable format.

**How to Request**: Use the "Export My Data" feature or email privacy@nexis.gov.in

### 5. Withdraw Consent
Stop using NEXIS at any time and request data deletion.

## Data Sharing

### We DO NOT:
- ❌ Sell your personal information to third parties
- ❌ Share your data with advertisers
- ❌ Use your data for marketing purposes
- ❌ Share your data with other government departments without consent

### We MAY Share:
- ✅ Anonymized, aggregated statistics for research
- ✅ Data with law enforcement if legally required
- ✅ Technical data with AWS for infrastructure management

## Cookies and Tracking

### What We Use:
- **Session Cookies**: To maintain your login session
- **Preference Cookies**: To remember your language choice
- **Analytics**: To understand how users interact with NEXIS

### What We DON'T Use:
- ❌ Third-party advertising cookies
- ❌ Cross-site tracking
- ❌ Social media tracking pixels

## Children's Privacy

NEXIS is designed for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## AI and Automated Decision Making

### How AI is Used:
- **Eligibility Checking**: Automated matching of your profile with scheme criteria
- **Explanations**: AI-generated explanations of eligibility decisions
- **Chat Assistant**: AI-powered responses to your questions

### Human Oversight:
- All AI responses are logged and monitored for quality
- Safety mechanisms prevent inappropriate or misleading responses
- You can always contact human support for clarification

### Your Rights:
- You have the right to understand how automated decisions are made
- You can request human review of any eligibility decision
- You can challenge automated decisions
