## ❓ Q&A PREPARATION

### Expected Questions & Answers

**Q: How accurate is your eligibility matching?**
> "Our engine evaluates 10+ criteria per scheme with 95%+ accuracy. We test against real government eligibility rules."

**Q: How do you handle data privacy?**
> "We use AWS encryption at rest and in transit. No PII is stored without consent. We're GDPR-compliant ready."

**Q: Can this scale to millions of users?**
> "Absolutely. We use AWS Lambda for serverless compute, DynamoDB for NoSQL storage, and CloudFront for CDN. Auto-scales to any load."

**Q: What about users without smartphones?**
> "We're voice-first. Users can call a toll-free number and speak naturally. Works on any phone, even feature phones."

**Q: How do you keep schemes updated?**
> "We have an admin portal for scheme managers to update rules. Plus automated scraping of government portals with AI validation."

**Q: What's your go-to-market strategy?**
> "Partner with CSC centers (3 lakh+ across India), integrate with UMANG app, and launch toll-free helpline. We can reach 100M users in 6 months."

**Q: How much does it cost to run?**
> "With AWS serverless, under $500/month for 1 million users. Scales linearly. Very cost-effective."

**Q: What makes you different from myScheme.gov.in?**
> "We add AI explanations, voice interface, match scoring, and personalized recommendations. We make it accessible to everyone, not just digitally literate users."
